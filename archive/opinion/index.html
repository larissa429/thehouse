THEY ARE NOT REPLACEMENTS

Over the past several days, many new residents have arrived
in the House.

Welcome them.

Help them learn where breakfast is served.
Help them find the Library.
Show them the Boardwalk.

But please…

do not ask anyone to believe that those we lost have somehow
returned.

The House may see a population restored.

We remember names.
