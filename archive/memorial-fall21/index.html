------------------------------------------------------------

This page has been established to preserve the names of
residents lost during the events of Summer 28.

Identification efforts have concluded for named residents.
The full number lost that day has never been confirmed —
many were pulled into the shoreline rift and could not be
recovered.

This page replaces the earlier notice posted Fall 7.
