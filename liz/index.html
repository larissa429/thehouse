SHE/HER 11 YEARS OLD WRITER VA NEEDED

Liz is Charlie's owner. She is very smart for her age and was pushed up a few grades in school because of it. In her free time, she enjoyed doing fun activities with Charlie, like dressing up, making art, and going on adventures. Liz has a good relationship with her parents, who made sure to comfort her deeply after Charlie disappeared. Liz now spends every moment waiting for her to turn up somewhere.

Voice notes

  • Liz is 11 — bright, articulate, and a little precocious, the kind of kid who has words for her feelings because she's a writer. She talks fast when she's excited and runs sentences together, fixating on specific details.
  • She should sound like a real kid, not a cartoon kid — grounded, warm, and genuine. Casting can be an actual child or an adult who can convincingly play one.
  • The everyday lines are about charm and energy; the final line is about restraint.
  • Most important: the grief should be quiet and a little stubborn, not theatrical. She's holding it together more than she should have to.
