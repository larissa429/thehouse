The Inbetween Times
Special Edition
Fall 1

------------------------------------------------------------

FESTIVAL ENDS IN TRAGEDY

More than one hundred residents are believed dead,
injured, or missing following last night's catastrophic
incident at the Boardwalk.

Search efforts continued through the night as volunteers
attempted to identify survivors and account for residents
separated during the evacuation.

The exact cause of the explosion remains unknown.

Several witnesses also report the appearance of a temporary
rift near the shoreline. Residents believed to have fallen
through have not been located.

The House has requested that residents avoid the beach until
further notice, via Journal. It has also said to have
implemented safety measures to prevent this from happening
in the future.

Additional updates will be published as information becomes
available.
