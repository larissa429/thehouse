IT/HE ~70 BLOOMS FROM THE DREAM GARDEN

Dream originates from the Dream Garden, a manifestation of residual memories of residents long gone from the Inbetween. Despite speaking very few words, simply being near him tends to make situations feel more quiet and manageable. It is usually something said simply and honestly when he does decide to speak, too. He reacts to events that others feel tense or strange with acceptance.

Trivia

  • Dream is the only one of its kind, made as a result of excess memories overflowing from the Dream Gardens one day. Though the release of excess memories is a regular occurance and helps maintain the health of the flora, it's never gained sentience before, and hasn't again since Dream was conceived.
  • Dream picked its own name. Creative, huh?
  • It doesn't need to eat, sleep, or seemingly breathe. It does anyways, especially when around -⁵⁄₂₈.
  • Dream prefers when others use 'it' to refer to it, or even just its name. Residents have a habit of using "he" though, which he doesn't correct.
  • Dream didn't have a favorite food until recently, when -⁵⁄₂₈ gave it a s'more to try for the first time.
