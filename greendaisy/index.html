SHE/THEY 1,900+ BLOOMS ONE OF SEVEN HEAD LIBRARIAN RUNS A TIGHT LIBRARY

Daisy, also known as D.A.I.S.Y. or Directory And Index System… Yes!, is the head librarian of the Library of All Pages. She is commanding and confident, and prefers to run the Inbetween's Library with absolute authority. She is loud when she needs to be, selective with her words, and unapologetically nitpicky about facts. However, she is not cruel, and her authority comes by through pride in her work. She is part of a hivemind of 7 total D.A.I.S.Y.s, all of which run the Library in collaboration.

Trivia

  • Green has never been late to start the Library's Quiet Hours, or late to close them.
  • She knows the exact location of every book, even those not reshelved yet.
  • She knows where most overdue books are, too.
  • She has never taken a day off in all 1900+ blooms, not even for the holidays. The other Daisies have stopped asking her to. Someone has to run the building.
  • Despite her demeanor, many residents prefer to run into Green versus some of the other Daisies.
