Games

A couple of ways to kill time in the Inbetween.

Trivia

The Quiz

50 questions on residents, lore, and things hidden around the site — how well do you actually know The House?

 Arcade

Household

Drop residents in and merge matching pairs into something bigger — see how far you can go before the room fills up.

 Strategy

Crossroads

A duel against The House on a 5×5 grid — you move along your column, it moves along its row. Score more points than it before either of you runs out of moves.

 Deduction

Who's in the House?

The House has picked a resident and isn't telling — ask yes/no questions to narrow it down, then take your guess.

 Cards

Uno

2–4 players against The House, Journal, and Mirror — match color or number, empty your hand first. House Rules mode adds slaps, stacking, and swaps.

 Arcade

Boardwalk Dash

Indigo's off again — jump the benches and lampposts as the Boardwalk scrolls by faster and faster. How far can you get?
