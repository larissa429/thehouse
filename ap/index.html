SHE/HER ~900 BLOOMS SPANISH VA NEEDED

Abstract Painting is dramatic, entitled, confident, and extremely theatrical. Her movement has been described by other residents as "a ballerina having a breakdown". She is used to being admired and becomes deeply offended when people don't give her the attention she wants or "interpret" her correctly. She thinks she is the main character in everyone's story, but is really just comedic relief in their eyes.

Connections

Trivia

  • Abstract Painting is originally from a small municipality in Toledo, Spain.
  • The man who painted her was a well known and highly respected artist across all of Eurasia.
  • Even though she speaks Spanish, the atmosphere of The Inbetween allows all of its visitors to comprehend as though they are fluent. AP can understand everyone else though she hears in other languages.
  • She will get in your business. That is a promise.

The voice claim is only a guide — you don't have to sound exactly like them. It's meant to show the character's general mood and tone.

Voice notes

  • AP speaks entirely in Spanish — the Inbetween's passive translation means other characters understand her naturally. Auditioners should be fluent Spanish speakers, comfortable performing in (preferably Castilian) Spanish.
  • Her delivery is theatrical and unhurried; she talks like someone who assumes everyone in the room is already hanging on her every word.
  • The conceited exterior is the performance — underneath it is someone deeply, quietly insecure.
  • Most important: the best moments are when those two things collide — the bravado slipping just slightly before she catches herself and overcorrects.

TALKING TO MIRROR
Sí, sí. Claro. Um... (deja de escuchar, interrumpe) Perdón, ¿qué? Vas a tener que repetirlo. O mejor no, en realidad. Me veo yo misma mejor cuando mantienes la boca cerrada.

Yes, yes. Quite. Um... (Stops listening, interrupts) Sorry, what? You're going to have to repeat that. Or don't, actually. I can see myself better when your mouth is closed.

TALKING TO HERSELF (REFLECTION)
(Pasando junto a su reflejo, se detiene) ¡Oh! Ahí estás. Hmph. Siento que no te había visto en siglos. ¿Qué... tres, cuatro pasos desde la última vidriera? (Suspira con nostalgia) Supongo que entraré a buscar un sombrero mientras estoy aquí. Sería trágico acabar desteñida por el sol como una impresión barata.

(Walking past her reflection, stops) Oh! There you are. Heh. I feel like I haven't seen you in forever. What... three, four steps since the last storefront window? (Sighs wistfully) I suppose I'll stop in and look for a hat while I'm here, it would be tragic if my canvas sun-faded like a cheap print...

TALKING TO HERSELF, ALONE IN HER ROOM
(Suspira) Nadie se recuerdo de mi Día de Llegada. Lo cual está... ¡bien! Hay muchos objetos aquí, y muchos Días de Llegada que celebrar. Aunque, claro, tendría sentido que yo estuviera al principio de la lista, considerando que... llevo aquí tanto tiempo, pero parece que apenas ocupo un rincón en sus pensamientos. (Pausa, solemne) Maldición, AP...

(Sighs) Nobody remembered my Arrival Day again. Which is... fine! There are a lot of objects here, and a lot of Arrival Days to celebrate. Of course, it would make sense if I were at the top of everyone's list, given... I've been here for so long, but it seems that I'm just in the back of their minds. (Pause, solemn) Damnit, AP...
