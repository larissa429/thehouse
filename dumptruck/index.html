SHE/HER ~3 BLOOMS LOVES TRASH (LITERALLY) PURE OF HEART VA NEEDED

Dumptruck is a newer resident of the House. She is dumb and stupid, but in a playfully genuine way rather than a negative one. Using her hollow back for storage and yellow scooper arm, she picks up any garbage she sees, which is also her favorite food. She likes to lighten the mood whenever she can as well as bond with others. Other residents who know her are well aware not to step into her room, as it is filled with trash she has collected.

Voice claim: Carolyn Lawrence

The voice claim is only a guide — you don't have to sound exactly like them. It's meant to show the character's general mood and tone.

Voice notes

  • Dumpy speaks with a thick, exaggerated southern drawl, a lisp, and boundless enthusiasm that never slows down. She's loud but never mean — think golden retriever energy.
  • Her sentences run together and she gets distracted mid-thought easily, often circling back to whatever excited her first. She mispronounces things confidently and without embarrassment.
  • Even her saddest moments should feel warm rather than heavy, like she doesn't fully understand what she's feeling but is trying her best anyway.
  • Most important: she is completely sincere at all times. There's no irony in her, ever. Think less "comedy character" and more "genuinely the most optimistic person in any room."
