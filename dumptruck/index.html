SHE/HER ~3 BLOOMS LOVES TRASH (LITERALLY)

Dumptruck is a newer resident of the House. She is dumb and stupid, but in a playfully genuine way rather than a negative one. Using her hollow back for storage and yellow scooper arm, she picks up any garbage she sees, which is also her favorite food. She likes to lighten the mood whenever she can as well as bond with others. Other residents who know her are well aware not to step into her room, as it is filled with trash she has collected.

Trivia

  • As she is a toy garbage truck, she doesn't have compacting abilities. This doesn't stop her from fitting as many goodies as she can find in her bed.
  • She only has one true arm. Her yellow side scooper serves as a second arm, though it can only move up and down on the Y-axis.
  • She doesn't like regular food, unless it's made to look unappetizing.
  • If her bedroom was a real junkyard, she would receive enough OSHA violations to create a path of papers to the moon and back.
  • The pointy tooth that sticks out of the left side of her mouth is the root of her lisp.
